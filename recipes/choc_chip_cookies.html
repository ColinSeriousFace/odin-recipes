Choc Chip Cookies

Description

You think you know chocolate chip cookies. But wait until you try these...

Ingredients

  • 100g of Butter
  • 75g of Sugar
  • 75g of light brown Sugar
  • 200g of Flour
  • 1 large egg
  • 1tsp of Vanilla Extract
  • 1/4 tsp of salt
  • 1 bar of Dark Chocolate, broken

Steps

  1. Melt the butter gently until almost completely melted.
  2. In a bowl, combine the (almost) melted butter with both sugars. Whisk them until fully incorporated.
  3. Add the egg, salt and Vanilla and whisk for about 15 seconds
  4. Then add the Flour and chocolate. Fold, don't whisk, the mixture together gently.
  5. Once incorporated, cover and place into a fridge for 3 to 24 hours.
  6. When you're done waiting, pre-heat your oven to 180C/160 fan.
  7. Remove from fridge and place scoops onto a lined baking tray.
  8. Put into oven and bake for 10-12 minutes or until edges firm but center still soft.
  9. Bon apple tea!
